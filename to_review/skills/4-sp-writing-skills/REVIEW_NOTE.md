## What it is

679 lines plus six supporting files, from `obra/superpowers`. How to write a skill: what belongs in one, how to name and describe it, how to test it with subagents, and a distilled set of Anthropic's own best practices. Ships a graph-rendering convention and a file on persuasion principles — how to word a rule so a model actually follows it.

## What we already have

`FACTORY_PHILOSOPHY.md` §3 and §5 are our answer to the same question: anatomy, the gates, the maturity ladder. We wrote ours from first principles and one cold-read test.

## What is worth taking

`testing-skills-with-subagents.md` above all — we invented the cold read independently and this is someone else's version of it, which either validates ours or improves it. Then `persuasion-principles.md`: our §7 says to explain why rather than issue bare imperatives, and this appears to be a worked treatment of the same problem. `anthropic-best-practices.md` is a second opinion on the skill anatomy.

## What to leave

Their file layout conventions if they conflict with ours. Ours is aimed at review by the owner; theirs may be aimed at distribution.
