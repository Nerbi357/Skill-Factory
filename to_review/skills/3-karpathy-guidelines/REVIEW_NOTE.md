## What it is

One small skill from `multica-ai`, built on Karpathy's observations about where LLMs fail: think before coding, simplicity first, surgical changes, goal-driven execution.

## What we already have

`4-working-agreement` covers the first three in his own words — understand before producing, decide alone versus ask, do not change what was not asked about.

## What is worth taking

The fourth: **give success criteria rather than instructions.** 'LLMs are exceptionally good at looping until they meet specific goals.' Nothing of ours frames a task that way, and it is a different shape from a procedure — it belongs near `4-verify-before-done`, which defines done but never says to state done *before* starting.

## What to leave

The first three, unless its wording is sharper than ours. Diff rather than adopt.
