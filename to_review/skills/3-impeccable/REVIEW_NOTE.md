# Review note — 3-impeccable

**Priority: 3/4** — A whole design system aimed at the exact failure the owner cares about: work that looks templated.

*Not in force. This note is a proposal; the verdict is the owner's.*

**Licence: Apache 2.0** — copying and reworking is permitted; attribution and a change notice must travel with any derivative.

## What it is

From `pbakaus`. One skill exposing 23 commands — shape, audit, critique, animate, typeset, polish — plus **59 deterministic detector rules** for design anti-patterns, and an init step that records product audience, branding, voice, colours and components for later commands to reference.

## What we already have

Nothing. `3-frontend-ui-engineering` and `4-frontend-design` are guidance; this is guidance plus mechanical checks.

## What is worth taking

The **59 deterministic detectors** — rules a machine can check rather than a model can judge. That is a different and stronger thing than a principle, and it is what would let `ux-designer` produce findings instead of opinions. Also the init step, which is `taste.md` under another name.

## What to leave

The 23-command surface. We have decided commands are skills with one flag, and importing a parallel command vocabulary would fight that.
